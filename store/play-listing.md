## Full description
Serenvox turns documents and text into smooth, natural speech using AI voices that run right on your phone.

🎙️ NATURAL AI VOICES, OFFLINE
Download free neural voices once (US and UK English, plus a pack with hundreds of voices) and listen anywhere, even in airplane mode. Your text is never uploaded.

▶️ PREVIEW BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD
Tap play on any voice to hear a sample before you download it, including featured voices from the multi-speaker pack.

📖 READ ANYTHING
• Import PDF, Word (.docx), EPUB and TXT files
• Scanned PDFs are read with on-device text recognition (OCR)
• Type or paste text
• Share text or files from any app with "Read aloud with Serenvox"
• Follow along: the current sentence is highlighted as it's read

🎧 LISTEN YOUR WAY
• Adjustable reading speed
• Sleep timer
• Lock-screen and notification controls; keeps reading in the background
• Falls back to your phone's built-in voice anytime

📚 LIBRARY
Your imported documents are kept in the Library for quick access.

💾 SAVE AND SHARE
Export speech to an audio file and share it.

🌙 Dark mode and Material You design.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Speech and text recognition happen on your device. No account, no sign-up.

💚 OPEN SOURCE

Learn more at https://serenvox.com/. Voice credits and open-source licences are listed in the app.
