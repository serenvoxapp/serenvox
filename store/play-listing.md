## Full description
Serenvox turns any text into smooth, natural speech using AI voices that run right on your phone.

🎙️ NATURAL AI VOICES, OFFLINE
Download free neural voices once (US and UK English, including a pack with hundreds of voices) and listen anywhere, even in airplane mode. Your text is never uploaded.

📖 READ ANYTHING
• Type or paste text, or import a .txt file
• Share text from any app with "Read aloud with Serenvox"
• Handles long articles and chapters smoothly, sentence by sentence
• Follow along: the current sentence is highlighted

🎧 LISTEN YOUR WAY
• Play, pause and stop, with lock-screen and notification controls
• Keeps reading in the background
• Adjust speed and pitch
• Falls back to your phone's built-in voice anytime

💾 SAVE AND SHARE
Export speech to a WAV audio file and share it.

🌙 Dark mode and Material You design.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Speech is generated on your device. No account, no sign-up.

Serenvox is free with ads. A one-time purchase removes all ads forever.

Voice credits and open-source licences are listed in the app.
