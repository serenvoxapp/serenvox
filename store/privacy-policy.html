Serenvox Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026

Serenvox ("the app") is a text-to-speech app developed by Ryan Lu (Toronto, Canada).

Your text and audio stay on your device

  • Text you type, paste, import or share into Serenvox is converted to speech entirely on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any speech service.
  • Exported audio files are created on your device and only shared where you choose.
  • We do not operate any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information.

    • Voice downloads

    When you download a voice, the app fetches the model file from GitHub (github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.). As with any download, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information. No text or personal data is sent.

    Advertising (free version)

    The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Advertising ID (and Android Privacy Sandbox ad signals where available),
  • device information (model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from IP address),
  • app interaction and diagnostic data (ad impressions, clicks, crash/performance logs).

    • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and applicable US states, the app asks for your consent/choices via Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy / ad consent options, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

    Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

    Purchases

    The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase is valid; we never see your payment details. After purchase, no ads are requested.

    Permissions

  • Internet: download voices, ads, and purchase verification.
  • Notifications / foreground service: show playback controls while speech plays in the background.

    • Children

    Serenvox is not directed at children under 13.
